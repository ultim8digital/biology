BIOLOGY HANDBOOK

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Biology Handbook is an offline educational biology reference and trainer published by Oleg Korunov. This policy describes the mobile app distributed through Google Play and the App Store. The same text is available inside the app, under About → Privacy.

The tutor, scanner, practice, flashcards and Biology Pro described below are introduced in version 1.1. Earlier versions contain the offline reference and calculators.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not create accounts and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not send us personal information and does not contact our servers.

What is stored on your device

The app’s local settings hold your chosen language, your theme, the list of articles you star for the cheat sheet, your practice results, the number of practice sets started today, your flashcard review schedule and a note that Biology Pro was purchased, so that Pro works offline. Numbers you type into a calculator, your conversation with the tutor and the text you type into search are processed on the device, stay in memory while the app is running, and are not saved to any history.

The reference works offline

The articles, worked examples, tables, translations and fonts are installed with the app. Search, the calculators, practice, flashcards, the cheat sheet, the constants and the unit tables all work without a network connection.

AI tutor and problem scanner

On iPhone and iPad, the tutor uses the Apple Intelligence language model, which runs on the device itself. Your questions, photos of problems and the answers never leave the device and are not kept after the app is closed. If a question needs to be translated, Apple’s offline on-device translation is used. The scanner recognises text on the device; the photo is not saved. The Android version has no tutor and no scanner.

Device permissions

On iPhone and iPad the camera turns on only when you choose to photograph a problem; the picture is recognised on the device and not saved. A photo from your library is picked in the system window, and the app receives only the photo you select. The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, files or location. The Android release requests no camera access.

Purchases, stores and the operating system

Biology Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app learns only whether the purchase has been made. Google Play, the App Store and your operating system handle installation, update, purchase and crash data under their own rules. That happens outside the app, and we do not receive that data.

Deleting your settings

To delete what the app has saved, clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Your Biology Pro purchase is not lost: the “Restore purchases” button brings it back. In the Android release, settings, favourites and progress are excluded from cloud backup and from transfer to a new device, so a reinstall starts from scratch. On iOS, local preferences may be included in the device backup that your system manages.

Children

The app is a study aid. It collects nothing from anyone, including children, and shows no advertising.

Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we receive your address and the contents of your message and use them only to reply. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Delivery is handled by your email provider and ours; this is separate from using the offline app.

Policy changes

Any change to the app’s data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
